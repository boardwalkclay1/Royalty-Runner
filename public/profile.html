How Your Profile Powers Royalty Runner

Your profile is the root identity for the entire app. Once saved, Royalty Runner uses this data to auto‑fill and connect modules:

  • Works: Uses your legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, and publisher on every song.
  • Contracts: Auto‑fills your name, email, PRO, and publisher into templates and red‑flag checkers.
  • Rights & Registration: Uses your PRO and IPI when you register works or claim royalties.
  • Documents: Tags files with your identity for quick search and export.
  • Royalties: Connects your PRO and publishing info to royalty destinations and tools.

All of this is stored in RRDB (a local database in your browser), so your data stays on your device while still powering the entire Royalty Runner ecosystem.
