Performance Royalty

Money earned when your music is performed publicly — radio, TV, live venues, streaming, bars, restaurants, and more.

Collected by PROs:

ASCAP BMI SESAC
Mechanical Royalty

Earned when your music is reproduced — streaming, downloads, CDs, vinyl.

The MLC
Publishing

The business side of your songwriting rights. Publishers register your works, collect royalties, and protect your compositions.

Publishing Tools
Neighboring Rights

Royalties paid to performers and master owners when recordings are played publicly in certain territories.

SoundExchange
PRO (Performance Rights Organization)

Organizations that collect performance royalties for songwriters and publishers.

ISRC

International Standard Recording Code — the unique ID for each sound recording.

Official ISRC Site
ISWC

International Standard Musical Work Code — the unique ID for the composition.

Split Sheet

A document listing all contributors to a song and their ownership percentages.

Split Sheet Template
Copyright

Legal protection for original works of authorship. Registration strengthens your rights and your ability to enforce them.
