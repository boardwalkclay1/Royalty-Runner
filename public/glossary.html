What This Glossary Is

The Royalty Runner Glossary is your personal dictionary for the entire music business. Every contract term, royalty type, legal phrase, publishing concept, and industry acronym is broken down in plain language — no gatekeeping, no confusion.

Use this page to understand deals, protect yourself, and move like a professional. Every term is searchable, filterable, and expandable.

How to Use It

  • Search any word or phrase using the search bar.
  • Tap a letter in the A–Z bar to filter alphabetically.
  • Tap a term to expand its definition and tags.
  • Use tags to understand which part of the industry it belongs to.
