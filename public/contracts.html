Contract Templates

Recording Agreement (Artist ↔ Label)

Covers master ownership, advances, recoupment, royalty rate, delivery, term, options, and territory.

Producer Agreement (Producer Points on Master)

Defines producer fee, points, recoupment, credit, stems delivery, and mix approvals.

Split Sheet (Songwriter / Composer Splits)

Documents ownership percentages of the composition, PRO info, IPI numbers, and admin rights.

Management Agreement (Artist ↔ Manager)

Covers commission %, term, territory, scope of services, expenses, and sunset clause.

Work‑for‑Hire Agreement

Used when you pay someone a flat fee and you own 100% of the results (masters or compositions).

Sync License (Music ↔ Film / TV / Content)

Grants the right to sync your master and/or composition to picture, with fee, term, territory, and media.
