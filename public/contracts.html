Types of Contracts

Recording Contract

Deal between artist and label for recording, releasing, and distributing music.

  • Ownership of masters
  • Advance & recoupment
  • Royalty rate & escalations
  • Term & options
  • Territory
Producer Agreement

Defines payment, ownership, royalties, and responsibilities between artist and producer.

  • Work‑for‑hire vs. royalty share
  • Points on the master
  • Delivery requirements
Split Sheet

Lists all contributors and their ownership percentages for a song.

Management Agreement

Defines the relationship between artist and manager.

  • Commission %
  • Term
  • Scope of services
  • Sunset clause
Publicist Agreement

Covers PR services, deliverables, timelines, and payment.

Work‑for‑Hire Agreement

Transfers ownership of the work to the hiring party.

Feature Verse Agreement

Defines terms when an artist features on another artist’s song.

Sync License Agreement

Grants permission to use music in film, TV, ads, games, etc.

Red‑Flag Checklist & Quick Comparison

Use this as a mental checklist before signing anything:

  • Is there a clear term (start/end date)?
  • Is the territory defined?
  • Who owns the masters and publishing?
  • Is there a perpetuity or “in perpetuity” clause?
  • Is there a clear royalty rate and recoupment language?
  • Is there a termination clause?
  • Is there a non‑compete or overly broad exclusivity?
Recording Deal
Label often owns masters, pays advance, recoups from royalties. Watch: recoupment, options, cross‑collateralization.
Distribution Deal
You usually keep masters, distributor takes % of revenue. Watch: term, fees, takedown rights.
Publishing Deal
Publisher collects and sometimes owns part of your songs. Watch: admin vs. full pub, term, reversion.
Work‑for‑Hire
You get paid once, no ownership. Watch: scope of work, credit, payment timing.
