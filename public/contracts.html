How This Page Works

This is your **drafting lane**, not long‑term storage. You load a template, it auto‑fills with your profile details where possible, you customize it, then:

  • Send it to the Documents Vault to store, sign, and version it
  • Email it as a draft for review or signature
  • Print a clean copy for in‑person signing

Any highlighted term is clickable — you’ll get a plain‑language explanation in a popup without leaving the page.

Contract Templates

Recording Agreement (Artist ↔ Label)
Use when a label is funding, releasing, or controlling your masters.

This covers:

  • Who owns the masters
  • Advances, recoupment, and royalty rates
  • Term, options, and territory
  • Delivery requirements and reversion / license options
Producer Agreement (Producer Points on Master)
Use when a producer is owed a fee and/or points on the master.

This covers:

  • Producer fee and points
  • Who owns the master and whether it’s work‑for‑hire
  • Credits, stems, and sample responsibilities
Split Sheet (Songwriter / Composer Splits)
Use every time more than one person writes a song.

This covers:

  • Songwriter names, roles, and percentages
  • PRO and IPI / CAE info
  • How mechanical and performance royalties are split
Management Agreement (Artist ↔ Manager)
Use when someone is managing your entire career.

This covers:

  • Commission on gross earnings
  • Term and sunset clause
  • Scope of services and expenses
Work‑for‑Hire Agreement
Use when you pay a flat fee and own 100% of the results.

This covers:

  • Clear “work‑for‑hire” language
  • Flat fee and payment schedule
  • Optional credit language
Sync License (Music ↔ Film / TV / Content)
Use when your music is being used with video or visuals.

This covers:

  • Media, territory, and term
  • Sync fee and credit language
  • Who controls the master and composition
Custom Contract (Blank Structured Template)
Use when you need something unique but still structured.

This gives you:

  • Clean sections for rights, term, compensation, and ownership
  • Signature blocks ready for printing or e‑signing

Active Contract Draft

Your profile details (legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, publisher) will auto‑fill into templates where possible. You can still edit anything manually.

Signing options: 1) Send to Documents and sign there (scan, upload, or e‑sign), 2) Email as a PDF or text for e‑signature, 3) Print and sign in person, then scan back into your Documents Vault.
