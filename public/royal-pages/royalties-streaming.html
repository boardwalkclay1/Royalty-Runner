Streaming Platforms (Audio)

How Streaming Royalties Work

Streaming platforms pay you when people listen to your music on their apps. Money flows to:

  • Master owner (via distributor)
  • Songwriters & publishers (via PRO + mechanical agencies)

Each platform has its own payout rate, rules, and territories. Below is a breakdown of the major and niche platforms where streams turn into money.

Global DSPs (Major Platforms)

Spotify

Spotify for Artists

Pays: Master royalties (via distributor) + publishing (via PRO/MLC).

Good for: All genres, playlists, algorithmic discovery, global reach.

Apple Music

Apple Music for Artists

Pays: Higher per-stream than many competitors, master + publishing.

Amazon Music

Amazon Music

Pays: Streams from Prime, Unlimited, and Alexa devices.

TIDAL

TIDAL

Pays: One of the highest per-stream rates, HiFi audience.

Deezer

Deezer

Pays: Streaming royalties, strong in Europe and Latin America.

Pandora (Premium)

Pandora

Pays: On-demand streams (master + publishing) via distributor.

YouTube Music

YouTube Music

Pays: Streaming royalties separate from YouTube video ad revenue.
