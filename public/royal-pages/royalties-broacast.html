Radio, Satellite & Broadcast Royalties

How Broadcast Royalties Work

Broadcast royalties are paid when your music is played on:

  • FM/AM radio
  • Satellite radio (SiriusXM)
  • Television networks
  • Local news stations
  • Cable channels
  • Sports broadcasts
  • International radio & TV

These royalties pay songwriters/publishers (via PROs) and in many countries also pay performers/master owners (via neighboring rights societies).

Television Networks (USA)

ABC / NBC / CBS / FOX

Uses: Music in shows, promos, news, sports, ads.

Pays: PRO royalties + sync fees (for placements).

CNN / MSNBC / Fox News

Uses: Bumpers, transitions, background beds.

Pays: PRO royalties.

ESPN / FS1 / NBC Sports

Uses: Sports highlights, intros, hype reels.

Pays: PRO royalties + sync for custom placements.

MTV / VH1 / BET

Uses: Music videos, shows, promos.

Pays: PRO royalties + sync.

HGTV / Food Network / Discovery

Uses: Background music in lifestyle shows.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Local TV Stations

Uses: News, weather, local programming.

Pays: PRO royalties.

International Radio & TV

BBC Radio (UK)

BBC

NRJ (France)

RTL / ARD / ZDF (Germany)

Latin American Radio Networks

Pays: Local PROs (SAYCO, SADAIC, ECAD, etc.).

African Radio Networks

Pays: SAMRO, CAPASSO, and local PROs.
