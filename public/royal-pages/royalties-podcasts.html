Podcast & Audiobook Royalties

How Podcasts Use Music

Podcasts use music for:

  • Intro themes
  • Outro themes
  • Background beds
  • Transitions
  • Sponsored segments
  • Storytelling soundtracks

These placements pay through sync fees, blanket licenses, and performance royalties when podcasts appear on platforms that track usage.
