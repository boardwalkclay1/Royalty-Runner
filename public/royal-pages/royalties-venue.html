Venue, Business & Public Space Royalties

How Public Performance Royalties Work

When your music is played in public spaces, PROs collect money from businesses and pay you:

  • Restaurants
  • Bars & clubs
  • Retail stores
  • Gyms & fitness centers
  • Hotels & resorts
  • Airlines
  • Cruise ships
  • Sports arenas
  • Malls & shopping centers
  • Theme parks

These royalties go to songwriters/publishers (via PROs) and sometimes performers/master owners (via neighboring rights societies).

Retail & Commercial Chains

Starbucks

Uses: Curated playlists in stores worldwide.

Pays: PRO royalties + licensing fees via playlist providers.

RetailGlobal
Target

Uses: In-store background music.

Pays: PRO royalties via blanket licenses.

Walmart

Uses: In-store radio + announcements.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Macy’s / Nordstrom / Bloomingdale’s

Uses: Retail playlists.

Pays: PRO royalties.

H&M / Zara / Uniqlo

Uses: Fashion retail playlists.

Pays: PRO royalties + licensing fees.

Restaurants, Bars & Clubs

Restaurant Chains

Examples: Chili’s, Applebee’s, Olive Garden, Outback, Red Lobster.

Pays: PRO royalties for background music.

Bars & Nightclubs

Uses: DJs, playlists, live performances.

Pays: PRO royalties + sometimes neighboring rights.

Sports Bars

Uses: Music + broadcast sports with licensed music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Breweries & Wineries

Uses: Background playlists + live music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Gyms, Fitness Centers & Wellness Spaces

Planet Fitness

Uses: Background playlists.

Pays: PRO royalties.

LA Fitness / 24 Hour Fitness

Uses: Workout playlists + classes.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Equinox

Uses: Curated high-end playlists.

Pays: PRO royalties + sync for branded content.

Yoga Studios

Uses: Ambient + meditation music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Hotels, Resorts & Hospitality

Marriott / Hilton / Hyatt

Uses: Lobby music, restaurants, lounges, spas.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Airbnb Hosts (Commercial)

Uses: Background playlists in rentals.

Pays: PRO royalties if classified as commercial.

Resorts & Casinos

Uses: Lounges, pools, restaurants, gaming floors.

Pays: PRO royalties + sometimes sync for shows.

Cruise Ships

Uses: Live shows, lounges, pool decks, restaurants.

Pays: PRO royalties + performance royalties.

Airlines & Transportation

Delta / American / United

Uses: Boarding music, in-flight playlists, branded videos.

Pays: Sync + PRO royalties.

JetBlue / Southwest

Uses: Boarding + in-flight music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Amtrak

Uses: Station + onboard music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Public Transit Systems

Uses: Station music, announcements, ads.

Pays: PRO royalties.

Sports Arenas & Stadiums

NBA Arenas

Uses: Warm-ups, timeouts, halftime shows.

Pays: PRO royalties + sync for broadcast.

NFL Stadiums

Uses: Pregame, halftime, hype music.

Pays: PRO royalties.

MLB / NHL / MLS Venues

Uses: In-game music + broadcast usage.

Pays: PRO + neighboring rights (international).

College Stadiums

Uses: Marching band arrangements + licensed music.

Pays: PRO royalties.
