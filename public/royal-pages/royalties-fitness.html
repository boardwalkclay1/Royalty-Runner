Fitness, Wellness & Lifestyle App Royalties

How Fitness & Wellness Apps Pay

Fitness, meditation, and lifestyle apps license music for:

  • Workout classes
  • Guided runs
  • Yoga sessions
  • Meditation tracks
  • Breathing exercises
  • VR fitness experiences

These placements pay through sync fees, blanket licenses, and performance royalties.
