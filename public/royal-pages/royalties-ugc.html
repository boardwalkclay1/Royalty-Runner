UGC Platforms & Creator Ecosystem Royalties

How UGC Royalties Work

User‑generated content platforms pay when creators use your music in:

  • Short videos
  • Livestreams
  • Memes & edits
  • Fan-made content
  • Reaction videos
  • Gaming clips

Payments flow through:

  • Usage royalties (platform → distributor → you)
  • Content ID claims (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Performance royalties (PROs)
  • Neighboring rights (international)
