Sync & Licensing Royalties

What is a Sync License?

A sync (synchronization) license is permission to use your music with visual media: film, TV, ads, trailers, games, apps, YouTube, TikTok, brand content, and more.

A single sync can generate:

  • Upfront fee (sync fee) for the master + composition.
  • Backend performance royalties when the content airs/broadcasts.
  • Extra exposure that drives streams, followers, and future deals.

Below are sync libraries, marketplaces, and companies that place music into visual media.
