Distributors & Aggregators

What a Distributor Actually Does

A distributor (or aggregator) is the pipeline between you and the platforms: Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Music, Amazon, Deezer, TIDAL, and hundreds more.

They:

  • Deliver your audio, artwork, and metadata to DSPs and social platforms.
  • Collect master royalties from streams, downloads, and some social uses.
  • Sometimes handle YouTube Content ID, sync pitching, and publishing admin.

Below is a breakdown of major distributors, what they cost, what they pay, and what extras they offer.
