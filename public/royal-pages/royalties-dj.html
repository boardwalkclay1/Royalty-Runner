DJ Pools, Mix Platforms & Club Royalties

How DJ Royalties Work

When DJs play your music in clubs, festivals, radio mixes, livestreams, or DJ pools, royalties flow through:

  • Performance royalties (via PROs)
  • Neighboring rights (international)
  • DJ pool payouts (some pools pay per download)
  • Mix platform royalties (Mixcloud, Apple DJ mixes)

Clubs and festivals pay blanket licenses to PROs, which then pay you for setlist usage.
