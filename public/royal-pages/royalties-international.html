International PROs & Collection Societies

Why PROs Matter Worldwide

Performance Rights Organizations (PROs) collect money when your music is performed publicly: radio, TV, venues, streaming, background music, and more.

If your music is played in another country, that country’s PRO usually collects first, then sends it to your home PRO, then to you.
