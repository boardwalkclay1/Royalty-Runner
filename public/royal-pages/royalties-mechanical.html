Mechanical Royalties

What Mechanical Royalties Are

Mechanical royalties are paid to songwriters and publishers when music is:

  • Streamed on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon, etc.
  • Downloaded (iTunes, Amazon MP3)
  • Manufactured physically (CDs, vinyl)
  • Used in karaoke, ringtones, and interactive media

These royalties are separate from master royalties and are collected by specialized agencies.
