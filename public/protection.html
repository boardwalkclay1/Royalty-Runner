This page walks you through everything you need to protect your music, name, and brand. If you don’t understand a term, the Royalty Runner Glossary teaches you everything you need to know.

1. Register Your Songs (Publishing & PROs)

Make sure every song is registered so it can earn performance and publishing royalties.

  • ASCAP – Writer/Publisher Registration
  • BMI – Song Registration
  • SESAC / PRS / SOCAN – Local PROs

2. Mechanical & Digital Royalties

Streaming and downloads generate mechanical royalties that must be claimed.

  • The MLC – US Mechanical Royalties
  • Harry Fox Agency
  • Your Digital Distributor Dashboard

3. Copyright Registration

Register your songs and recordings with your country’s copyright office.

  • US Copyright Office – Register a Work

4. Trademarks & Brand Protection

Protect your artist name, logo, and brand so no one else can claim them.

  • USPTO – Trademark Search & Filing

5. Neighboring Rights & Masters

Get paid when your recordings are played on radio, TV, and overseas.

  • SoundExchange – US Digital Performance
  • PPL / Other CMOs – International

6. Business & Agreements

Lock in your splits and protect yourself with clear agreements.

  • Split sheets for every song
  • Work‑for‑hire agreements when you pay others
  • Consider forming an LLC for your music business
