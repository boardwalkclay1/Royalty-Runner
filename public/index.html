The Independent Artist’s Survival Kit

Royalty Runner is built for every artist who ever felt lost, overlooked, or unprepared in the music industry. It’s your offline-first, cinematic command center for rights, royalties, contracts, management, and knowledge.

What Royalty Runner Really Is

A complete rights & royalty education

Every royalty type, every organization, every form, every registration — broken down in plain language. No gatekeeping. No confusion. No industry secrets.

A management team in your pocket

Booking tools, publicist tools, budgeting, touring, release planning, and strategy — all in one place. Built so independent artists can move like they have a full team.

A legal & contract safety net

Artist-friendly contracts, red flag warnings, deal breakdowns, and a glossary that explains every term you’ll ever see in a contract.

A catalog & document vault

Store your works, metadata, stems, memos, contracts, invoices, and legal files — all offline, all private, all yours.

A tool built from real experience

Royalty Runner was created by an artist — a skater — who was approached by the industry without guidance, without knowledge, and without anyone to teach him the business. This app exists so no independent artist ever has to feel that lost again.
