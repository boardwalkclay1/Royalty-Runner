GET PAID FOR YOUR MUSIC

You are supposed to get paid every time your music is played. Your art should be paid every time it’s displayed — just like everyone else that you hear, on video games, in restaurants and at the gym. Let me show you how.

For rappers, singers, producers, beatmakers, engineers, composers, and musicians of every kind.

Most artists are not getting paid because the money has nowhere to land. Streams, placements, and performances generate revenue — but without correct registrations and payment routing, those earnings never reach you. Check the Rights & Registration page to learn where royalties come from and how to claim them.
