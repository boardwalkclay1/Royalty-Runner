Contracts, Red Flags, and Legal Protection

Every artist eventually signs something — a split sheet, a beat lease, a management deal, a distribution agreement, or even a simple collaboration message. Understanding contracts isn’t optional; it’s survival. Most bad deals don’t look dangerous at first, and the biggest red flags are usually hidden in friendly language. When you know how to read terms, spot traps, and understand your rights, you protect your future royalties, your ownership, and your freedom as an artist. This section exists so you never walk into a deal blind again.

Your Works, Metadata, and Catalog

Your catalog is your legacy — every song, stem, memo, lyric, and idea becomes part of the story you’re building. Artists lose money every day because their files are scattered, metadata is missing, or they can’t prove when something was created. Keeping your works organized, timestamped, and stored privately gives you control over your creativity and your business. When your catalog is clean, you can register songs faster, pitch confidently, and protect your ownership with real proof.

Document Vault

Every artist eventually collects important documents — contracts, invoices, agreements, EPKs, release forms, and legal proof of ownership. Losing even one of these can cost you money, opportunities, or protection. A secure offline vault keeps everything in one place, under your control, with no third‑party access. When you can instantly pull up any document, you move like a professional, protect your rights, and stay ready for managers, labels, lawyers, or collaborators.
