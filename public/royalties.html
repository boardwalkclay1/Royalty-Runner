Free Platforms (Upload & Earn)

YouTube Content ID (Audio & Video)

YouTube pays royalties through ads and Content ID.

Pays: Ad revenue + Content ID claims.

Use it for: Official videos, visualizers, lyric videos, beat videos, and background music.

SoundCloud Monetization

SoundCloud Premier pays creators for streams.

Pays: Fan-powered royalties.

Use it for: Early drops, demos, remixes, and building a core fanbase.

Audiomack AMP

Audiomack AMP pays for streams.

Pays: Streaming royalties.

Use it for: Hip-hop, Afrobeats, and global street music discovery.

TikTok Sounds

TikTok pays when your sound is used in videos.

Pays: Usage-based royalties.

Use it for: Hooks, snippets, challenges, and viral moments.

Facebook & Instagram Music

Meta Music pays for music used in Reels, Stories, and posts.

Pays: Usage royalties.

Use it for: Short-form content, fan clips, and cross-posting your TikTok sounds.

Bandcamp

Bandcamp lets fans pay you directly for downloads, merch, and more.

Pays: Direct sales + Bandcamp Friday boosts.

Use it for: Albums, deluxe editions, stems, and supporter-only content.

Twitch & Live Streaming

Twitch and other live platforms pay through subs, bits, and donations.

Pays: Direct fan support + some music rights payouts.

Use it for: Live sets, studio sessions, Q&As, and beat showcases.

Patreon / Membership

Patreon and similar platforms let fans subscribe monthly.

Pays: Monthly recurring income.

Use it for: Early access, behind-the-scenes, stems, writing sessions, and private drops.

Paid Distributors (Get on Spotify, Apple, etc.)

DistroKid

DistroKid uploads your music to Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, and 150+ platforms.

Pays: Streaming + mechanical royalties.

Notes: Annual fee, unlimited releases, good for high output.

TuneCore

TuneCore distributes to all major platforms.

Pays: Streaming + downloads.

Notes: Per-release or annual plans, strong admin tools.

CD Baby

CD Baby distributes your music and can collect publishing royalties.

Pays: Streaming + sync + publishing.

Notes: One-time fee per release, long-tail catalog focus.

UnitedMasters

UnitedMasters distributes and offers brand/sync opportunities.

Pays: Streaming + brand deals.

Notes: Good for artists aiming at brand partnerships.

Amuse / Ditto / Others

There are many more distributors with different pricing and features.

Use this page as a map and then research which one fits your release strategy.

Sync Licensing (Film, TV, Ads, Games)

Pond5

Pond5 is a marketplace for music, sound effects, and video licensing.

Pays: 40%–60% per license.

Motion Array

Motion Array pays contributors monthly based on downloads.

Pays: $200–$2,000/month (varies heavily).

Artlist

Artlist licenses music for film and commercial use.

Pays: Monthly revenue share.

Epidemic Sound

Epidemic Sound pays upfront + streaming royalties.

Pays: Upfront + streaming + bonuses.

Musicbed / PremiumBeat / Others

High-end sync libraries focused on film, trailers, and branded content.

Pays: Per-license fees + backend royalties (depending on deal).

Beyond Streaming: Fitness, Podcasts, Venues & More

Fitness & Wellness Apps

Peloton, Apple Fitness+, and similar platforms license music for workouts.

Pays: Sync + performance royalties.

Podcasts & Audiobooks

Music in podcasts and audiobooks can generate sync + performance royalties.

Tip: Use podcast music libraries or pitch directly to creators.

Venues & Public Spaces

Bars, clubs, restaurants, gyms, and stores pay blanket fees to PROs.

Pays: Performance royalties via your PRO.

Radio & Broadcast

Terrestrial and internet radio spins generate performance royalties.

Tip: Register your works with your PRO and SoundExchange.

UGC Platforms & DJ Pools

DJ pools, remix platforms, and UGC sites can generate both exposure and royalties.

Tip: Always keep your writer and publisher info correct.
