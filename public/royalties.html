Understanding Royalties

Royalties are payments you earn when your music, vocals, beats, or videos are used, streamed, downloaded, licensed, or placed in media.

There are 4 main royalty types:

  • Performance Royalties — paid when your music is played publicly (radio, TV, venues, streaming).
  • Mechanical Royalties — paid when your music is reproduced (streaming, downloads, CDs).
  • Sync Royalties — paid when your music is used in film, TV, ads, games, YouTube, etc.
  • Neighboring Rights — paid to performers & master owners for international plays.

Every royalty type has its own systems, agencies, and platforms. This guide shows you every place you can upload, earn, and collect.

Free Platforms (Upload & Earn)

YouTube Content ID (Audio & Video)

YouTube pays royalties through ads and Content ID.

Pays: Ad revenue + Content ID claims.

Rate: $1–$4 per 1,000 views (varies heavily).

Good for: Beats, songs, instrumentals, tutorials, music videos.

SoundCloud Monetization (Audio)

SoundCloud Premier pays creators for streams.

Pays: Fan-powered royalties.

Rate: $2–$4 per 1,000 streams.

Audiomack AMP (Audio)

Audiomack AMP pays for streams.

Pays: Streaming royalties.

Rate: $1–$5 per 1,000 streams.

TikTok Sounds (Audio)

TikTok pays when your sound is used in videos.

Pays: Usage-based royalties via distributor.

Rate: $0.03–$0.06 per use.

Facebook & Instagram Music (Audio)

Meta Music pays for music used in Reels, Stories, and posts.

Pays: Usage royalties via distributor.

Paid Distributors (Get on Spotify, Apple, etc.)

DistroKid

DistroKid uploads your music to Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, and 150+ platforms.

Cost: $22.99/year.

Pays: Streaming + mechanical royalties.

Spotify Rate: $3–$5 per 1,000 streams.

TuneCore

TuneCore distributes to all major platforms.

Cost: $14.99/year.

Pays: Streaming + downloads.

CD Baby

CD Baby distributes your music and collects publishing royalties.

Cost: $9.99 per single.

Pays: Streaming + sync + publishing.

Sync Licensing (Film, TV, Ads, Games)

Pond5 (Audio & Video)

Pond5 is a marketplace for music, sound effects, and video licensing.

Pays: 40%–60% per license.

Good for: Beats, cinematic music, sound effects, vocals, video clips.

Motion Array (Audio & Video)

Motion Array pays contributors monthly based on downloads.

Pays: $200–$2,000/month depending on downloads.

Good for: Beats, sound effects, loops, video assets.

Artlist (Audio)

Artlist licenses music for film and commercial use.

Pays: Monthly revenue share.

Epidemic Sound (Audio)

Epidemic Sound pays upfront + streaming royalties.

Pays: Upfront + streaming + usage bonuses.
