What a Manager Really Does

A music manager is the architect of an artist’s career. They coordinate opportunities, negotiate deals, build relationships, protect the artist’s time, and guide every major decision. A great manager is part strategist, part negotiator, part business partner, and part shield.

Managers handle:

  • Booking shows, tours, and appearances
  • Negotiating performance fees and contracts
  • Coordinating releases, marketing, and timelines
  • Building industry relationships
  • Managing budgets, revenue, and expenses
  • Protecting the artist from bad deals and bad actors
  • Overseeing branding, image, and long-term strategy
  • Connecting the artist with lawyers, publicists, and agencies

This page gives you the tools to manage your own career with the same clarity, structure, and professionalism as a full management team.

The Publicist Command Center

A publicist controls the narrative. They shape how the world sees you, how the media talks about you, and how your brand grows. Publicists are responsible for:

  • Press releases
  • Media outreach
  • Brand image and storytelling
  • Interview preparation
  • Press kits (EPKs)
  • Social media strategy
  • Crisis management
  • Securing blog, radio, and playlist placements
