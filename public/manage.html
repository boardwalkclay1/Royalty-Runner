What a Manager Really Does

A manager is the architect of an artist’s career — strategist, negotiator, business partner, and shield.

  • Booking shows, tours, and appearances
  • Negotiating performance fees and contracts
  • Coordinating releases, marketing, and timelines
  • Building industry relationships
  • Managing budgets, revenue, and expenses
  • Protecting the artist from bad deals and bad actors
  • Overseeing branding and long-term strategy
  • Connecting the artist with lawyers, publicists, and agencies

Booking Shows & Touring

Managers secure shows, negotiate fees, and build touring schedules.

  • Sonicbids – Booking Platform
  • ReverbNation – Gig Finder
  • Bandsintown – Tour Tools

Booking Checklist

  • Confirm venue capacity and age restrictions
  • Negotiate performance fee and payment schedule
  • Review contract terms and cancellation clauses
  • Confirm sound requirements and stage plot
  • Send tech rider, hospitality rider, and EPK
  • Verify backline availability
  • Arrange travel and lodging
  • Promote the event
  • Collect payment details
  • Confirm load‑in time, soundcheck, and set length
