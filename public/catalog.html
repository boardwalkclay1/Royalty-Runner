Royalty Definitions

Performance Royalties – Earned when your music is played publicly (radio, TV, venues, streaming). Collected by PROs like ASCAP, BMI, SESAC.

Mechanical Royalties – Earned when your music is reproduced (streaming, downloads, CDs). Collected by agencies like The MLC.

Publishing Royalties – Earned from the songwriting/composition side. Publishers and administrators collect these globally.

Neighboring Rights – Earned by performers and master owners when recordings are played publicly overseas.

Digital Distributor – Platforms like DistroKid, TuneCore, and UnitedMasters that deliver your music to streaming services.
