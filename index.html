Royalty Runner

A free, browser‑based royalty assistant for up‑and‑coming artists.

Profile Works Royalty Forms Royalty Catalog A–Z Exports

One place to prepare your music for royalties.

Royalty Runner is a completely static, in‑browser app designed specifically for new and independent artists. It helps you organize your profile, your songs, and your royalty registrations—without logins, subscriptions, or servers.

Everything runs locally using IndexedDB. Your data never leaves your device. You can use Royalty Runner to auto‑fill forms for performance royalties, mechanical royalties, digital performance royalties, and more. When you’re done, you export what you need and keep moving.

What Royalty Runner does

  • Profile: Store your legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, publisher, and contact info.
  • Works: Add songs with titles, ISRCs, splits, and release dates.
  • Auto‑fill: Generate pre‑filled royalty forms using your profile and works.
  • Catalog: Browse an A–Z guide to royalty organizations with clear explanations.
  • Exports: Prepare data for PDFs, copy‑paste forms, and submission checklists.

Why it’s easy

  • No account: No sign‑up, no password, no email required.
  • No backend: Everything is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB.
  • No jargon: Plain‑language explanations for every royalty organization.
  • Beginner‑friendly: Built for artists who are just starting to learn the business.

Why it’s one‑of‑a‑kind

Most royalty tools are built for labels, publishers, or advanced teams. Royalty Runner is built for you—the artist who is still figuring out what ASCAP, BMI, SoundExchange, the MLC, and YouTube Content ID even are. It doesn’t just collect your data; it teaches you what each step means and why it matters.

You can use it as a private prep space: fill out your profile, add your songs, generate forms, and then submit them directly on the official sites.

How your data is stored

Royalty Runner uses your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB). Your data is not backed up or synced anywhere. If you clear your browser data, switch devices, or delete your profile inside the app, your information will be permanently removed.

This is intentional: you stay in control, and nothing leaves your device.
