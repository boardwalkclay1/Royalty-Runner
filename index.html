Own your rights. Control your royalties. Protect your art.

Royalty Runner is a fully static, offline‑ready rights and royalty assistant built for new and independent artists. It stores your information locally, auto‑fills industry forms, organizes your catalog, and teaches you the business behind your music.

What you can do

Build your profile

Your artist profile is the foundation of everything. Royalty Runner uses your legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, publisher, and contact info to auto-fill every form across the platform.

Store your works

Save titles, ISRCs, splits, release dates, collaborators, and metadata. This powers your registrations, royalty forms, and catalog exports.

Go to Works
Auto‑fill royalty forms

Instantly generate pre-filled text for ASCAP, BMI, SoundExchange, MLC, YouTube CID, and dozens of other organizations using your saved profile + works.

Go to Royalties
Explore Rights & Registration

A complete A–Z directory of royalty organizations with official links, auto-fill tools, and explanations of what each one does.

Manage your career

Learn the law
