Own your rights. Control your royalties. Protect your art.

Royalty Runner is a fully static, offline‑ready rights and royalty assistant built for new and independent artists. It stores your information locally, auto‑fills industry forms, organizes your catalog, and teaches you the business behind your music.

No accounts. No servers. No tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What you can do

  • Build your profile: Legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, publisher, and more.
  • Store your works: Titles, ISRCs, splits, release dates, notes.
  • Auto‑fill royalty forms: ASCAP, BMI, SoundExchange, MLC, YouTube CID, and more.
  • Manage your catalog: Ownership, metadata, splits, and registrations.
  • Learn the law: Copyright, publishing, royalties, contracts, and artist rights.
  • Use real templates: Contracts, agreements, notices, and protection documents.
  • Explore Rights & Registration: A–Z directory with official links and guidance.
  • Export your data: Profile, works, and forms as JSON backups.

Built for new artists

  • No account needed: No email, no password, no signup.
  • Local‑only: Your data never leaves your device.
  • Artist‑friendly language: No legal jargon, no confusion.
  • Offline‑ready: Works even without internet once loaded.

Your data stays with you

Royalty Runner uses IndexedDB to store your profile, works, and generated forms. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Clearing your browser data will permanently remove your information.

Export your data regularly if you want to keep backups.
