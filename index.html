Royalty Runner

A local, offline‑ready royalty prep space for up‑and‑coming artists.

Profile Works Royalty Forms Royalty Catalog Exports

Your private royalty command center.

Royalty Runner is a completely static, browser‑based app built for new and independent artists. It helps you collect the information every royalty organization asks for, store it locally in your browser, and auto‑fill forms so you can register your songs faster.

There are no logins, no servers, and no accounts. Everything runs on your device using IndexedDB. When you clear your browser data, your information is gone. Until then, this app is your private prep space.

What you can do here

  • Profile: Save your legal name, stage name, PRO, IPI, publisher, and contact info.
  • Works: Add songs with titles, ISRCs, splits, and release dates.
  • Auto‑fill: Generate pre‑filled text for ASCAP, BMI, SoundExchange, the MLC, and more.
  • Catalog: Browse an A–Z guide to royalty organizations with direct links to official sites.
  • Exports: Export your profile, works, and generated forms as JSON for your own backups.

Built for new artists

  • No account: No email, no password, no signup.
  • No backend: All data is stored locally in your browser.
  • Plain language: Explanations are written for artists, not lawyers.
  • Offline‑ready: Once loaded, the app can work without a connection.

How your data works

Royalty Runner uses your browser’s IndexedDB to store your profile, works, and generated forms. Nothing is uploaded or synced. If you clear your browser data, switch devices, or uninstall your browser, your information will be permanently removed.

This keeps your data private and under your control, but it also means you are responsible for exporting anything you want to keep.
